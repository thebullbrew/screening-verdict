The Method & The Honest Caveats

Score (100 pts): credit 35, income coverage 25, rental history 20 (evictions and late payments bite here), job stability 10, collections 10. Each band is interpolated, not cliff-edged — a 584 is not magically different from a 586. Default risk is estimated from the score (about 3% at 90+, 9% at 70, 24% at 50, 48% at 30) and priced against one bad outcome: eviction cost + 2.5 months of unpaid rent + turnover. Waiting is priced at the rent you forgo each day, plus the next (better) applicant's smaller risk. Money already burned while vacant is shown as sunk — it should sting, but it should not decide. Probabilities are planning estimates, not predictions about a person. Courts, timelines and deposit rules vary by state; price your own eviction cost honestly.
